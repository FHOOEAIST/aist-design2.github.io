{% comment %} Year heading of the publication / thesis listings, as a collapsible group. Expects: include.year — the year to print include.first — true for the first heading (nothing to close before it) Emits the closing tag of the previous year's `.year-group`, the heading with its toggle button, and the opening tag of this year's group; the caller closes the last group after its loop. Entries are `.listing-item` children of the group. main.js (initYearGroups) wires the button and, after every filter run, opens the newest visible year and collapses the rest; without JS every year stays open. {% endcomment %} {%- unless include.first -%}{%- endunless -%}
